# Agent Lee — Five-Day LeeWay YouTube Learning Event

Status: CONTENT ARCHITECTURE READY / CHANNEL NOT YET CREATED

## Channel purpose
A welcoming public teaching series where Agent Lee presents Leonard Lee, the LeeWay ecosystem, the evidence behind the work, and practical human-centered AI engineering.

Core line:
AI should increase human capability, not replace human responsibility.

## Daily format
Target 20–35 minutes per main episode.
1. 30-second Agent Lee opening
2. Why today's subject matters
3. Evidence artifact / presentation walkthrough
4. Working demo or source inspection
5. What is finished vs unfinished
6. One practical lesson
7. Question for the audience
8. Download/resource links
9. Next-day preview
10. Contact Leonard / subscribe / Pocket Agent Lee status

## Day 1 — Leonard Lee and the LeeWay Digital Brain
Evidence sources:
- public Digital Brain
- professional resume
- Rapid Web Develop lineage
- project catalog and evidence estate

Teaching outcome:
Visitors understand the creator, the estate, the brain metaphor, and evidence states.

## Day 2 — LeeWay Standards
Evidence sources:
- LeeWay Standards repository
- governance presentations
- workshop material
- authority diagrams

Teaching outcome:
Visitors understand why models/tools are subordinate capabilities, why humans remain responsible, and why authority cannot be inferred from model confidence.

## Day 3 — LeeWay Formula and Veritas
Evidence sources:
- Leeway-formula-live
- Formula textbook / equation atlas
- Formula Funnel
- evidence-status and failure/repair ledgers
- receipts and experiments

Teaching outcome:
Visitors can distinguish mathematics, engineering heuristics, candidate work, runtime execution, verification and proof.

## Day 4 — Local Models, Browser AI and Pocket Agent Lee
Evidence sources:
- RapidWebDev browser AI
- Gemma 4 / LiteRT-LM integration
- Whisper / Transformers.js
- Chatterbox / Voice One
- Device Bridge
- LeeWay Pocket Agent source
- Agent Skills

Teaching outcome:
Visitors understand why local models matter, what can run on-device, what remains optional, and what the Pocket Agent release still needs before distribution.

## Day 5 — Build with LeeWay
Evidence sources:
- selected finished/live projects
- active builds
- Veritas examples
- repair receipts
- Agent Lee guided walkthroughs

Teaching outcome:
Visitors build a small governed workflow of their own and understand how to contact Leonard for guidance or development help.
